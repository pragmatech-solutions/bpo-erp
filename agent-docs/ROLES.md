## ROLES

1. Three types of users, admin, team-lead, and agent

### AGENT ROLE

Basic features, an agent can only create leads, and then he can see their status only. He can only deal with his own leads. Nothing else.

### TEAM LEAD ROLE

Currently not avaliable, he would be added in future

### ADMIN ROLE

Admin can see all the leads, and update them with billable and non billable status. When he is making a lead non-billable, he must provide a reason for it.
